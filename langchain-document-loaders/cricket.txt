### The Spirit of Cricket

Under the sun, the green fields glow,
The players walk in, steady and slow.
A bat in hand, a dream in sight,
They step onto the pitch to fight.

The bowler runs with fire and pace,
The batter stands with fearless grace.
A thunderous shot, the crowd goes wild,
For one brief moment, the game has smiled.

A spinning ball, a swinging seam,
Every delivery carries a dream.
A diving catch, a wicket falls,
And echoes rise from stadium walls.

But cricket is more than runs and fame,
It teaches courage, patience, and game.
Win or lose, through joy or pain,
You rise, you learn, and play again.

So let the bat and ball collide,
Let passion and hope stand side by side.
For every match and every fight,
Cricket keeps the spirit bright.
